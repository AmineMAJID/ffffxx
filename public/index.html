ZIP signatures are detected directly. FXAP input is decoded with its container layout, plus an optional content key for a second layer.

02

Careful in-memory extraction

Entries are checked for unsafe paths, duplicates, encryption, symlinks, and excessive sizes before they enter your workspace.

03

A fresh, valid ZIP

Removed entries are omitted when your new archive is built. The source upload is never modified, and reset is always available.

A quick note about .fxap

A FiveM .fxap descriptor is usually protected metadata—not a ZIP file. When decoded data is not a ZIP, FXAP Studio says so and offers the exact decoded bytes for download. It does not retrieve Keymaster grants, bypass entitlements, or decompile Lua.
